01 — Profile

A dual discipline. One lens.

I am interested in the way accounting and finance help explain how a business is really performing. On the accounting side, I like the structure behind GAAP, reconciliations, retained earnings, and financial statements that actually tie. On the finance side, I enjoy using those numbers to model cash flows, test assumptions, and make stronger business decisions.

At UCF, I’m pursuing a dual B.S. in Accounting & Finance (3.6 GPA, December 2027), followed by a planned M.S. in Accounting — putting me on track for CPA eligibility in December 2028. Right now, I’m a Staff Accountant Intern at Kerby Accounting & Business Solutions, where I prepare individual and business tax returns, reconcile accounts for clients with up to $10M in revenue, and have supported buy-side due diligence work. Outside of class, I serve as VP of Pledge Education & Finance Chair for Delta Sigma Pi’s 100+ member chapter and helped launch UCF’s student-run consulting group as an inaugural board member.

The work I’m most proud of is the kind that makes things clearer: a three-statement model that balances across projected years, a due-diligence review that caught omitted payroll expenses before a client’s acquisition, and a CRM tracker that replaced a manual process entirely.
