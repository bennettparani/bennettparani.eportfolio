About

I am interested in how accounting and finance explain the way a business is really performing. On the accounting side, I like the structure behind GAAP, reconciliations, and financial statements that actually tie. On the finance side, I enjoy using those numbers to model cash flows, test assumptions, and support better decisions.

Today I work as a Staff Accountant Intern at Kerby Accounting & Business Solutions, where I analyze financial statements on due diligence and advisory engagements, clean up and reconcile client books, and prepare business and individual tax returns. I study Accounting and Finance at the University of Central Florida and am working toward the CPA.

Tie everything out

A reconciliation is not finished until it balances. I once traced an $11 difference across more than 2,000 transactions back to its source.

Look for what is missing

In due diligence, the number that matters most can be the one that is not there, like payroll expenses left out of a target’s reported earnings.

Replace manual work

When a process is repetitive, I build a tool for it. My CRM tracker for Knights Consulting Group cut outreach tracking time by about 40%.
